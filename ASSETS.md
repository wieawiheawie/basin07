# Future asset swaps

Milestone 1 uses procedural geometry only. No external model is required to run it, and no third-party files have been copied into this repository.

These official Kenney source pages were checked and each named pack page identifies its licence as **Creative Commons CC0**:

- [Kenney Furniture Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit) — a possible source for later room and desk furniture replacements.
- [Kenney Prototype Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/prototype-kit) — a possible source for simple character and prop replacements.
- [Kenney support and licensing notes](https://kenney.nl/support) — licensing information for Kenney's asset packs.

Check the downloaded pack's included licence file and available export formats. Prefer GLB or glTF, and keep a copy of the licence information with any imported pack.

Nothing is downloaded yet. Keep the procedural objects working until a replacement has been imported and checked in the scene.

Quaternius is deliberately not listed as a CC0 source: its current [licence page](https://quaternius.com/license.html) identifies the Quaternius Asset License (QAL), not CC0. Review that licence separately before choosing its files.
